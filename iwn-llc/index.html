About iWN

From advanced research to deployable systems

iWN is a U.S.-based technology company focused on converting advanced research in networking, AI, digital twins, and cyber-physical systems into deployable software and validation capabilities for government, scientific, and industrial customers.

The company emphasizes platform-independent architectures, auditable decision-making, robust operation under degraded conditions, and practical integration with existing hardware and orchestration systems.

Core focus areas
  • Trustworthy and distributed AI
  • Resilient wireless and edge networking
  • Digital twins and hardware-in-the-loop validation
  • Multi-agent coordination and resource orchestration
  • Autonomous-system assurance and recovery
Technology

Integrated trust, orchestration, and validation

iWN combines real-time sensing, network and resource awareness, machine-checkable planning, digital-twin validation, and replayable evidence to improve the reliability and scalability of autonomous and AI-driven systems.

Trust & Resilience

TRUST-Core

A platform-agnostic runtime for confidence estimation, anomaly detection, resilient collaboration, and evidence logging across RF, PNT, networking, and autonomous-system data.

Autonomous Laboratories

TRUST-Lab

A verification and recovery layer for AI-driven laboratories that checks plan feasibility, monitors instrument and infrastructure conditions, supports bounded recovery, and creates auditable execution records.

Validation

Digital-Twin & HIL Tooling

Scenario generation, fault injection, telemetry replay, and hardware-in-the-loop testing for evaluating distributed AI, networked autonomy, and resilient cyber-physical systems.

Product Strategy

Software, integration, and validation offerings

Runtime Software

Deployable software for trust estimation, orchestration, anomaly awareness, recovery, resource coordination, and evidence capture.

Integration SDK

APIs, adapters, telemetry schemas, test harnesses, and reference integrations for connecting iWN software with customer platforms and existing orchestration systems.

Validation-as-a-Service

Structured evaluation campaigns using simulation, digital twins, HIL, fault injection, scenario replay, acceptance testing, and engineering support.
